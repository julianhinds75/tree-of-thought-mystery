# Chapter 3 Murphy Manor

A long pebbled driveway that lead into the main garden, along the path was a small building, large enough to be a servants quarters. Perfectly maintained hedges lined manor. A huge two storied building split into 2 wings that were separated by the main entrance. 
In the coach Paige makes a comment about how the manor reminds her of her uncle Peter’s  summer house in York. Shocked, Walter says that he didn’t know that Paige was rich, she corrects him “My family is WEALTHY, Walter. You have the potential to become rich through a successful law career, but do you have an employer?”
“Well yes of course” Walter replied.”
Well that person Walter, is wealthy” Paige replied as she observed the grounds as the coach approached the front of the manor.

Outside stood a tall athletic man in his late 50’s, clean shaven, perfectly cut hair carefully made his way down the short staircase. He wore a butlers attire, black suit, white tie and immaculately polished shoes that caught the light of the mid morning sun. He had an expression that was undreadable. As the carriage pulled up at the house, Walter whispered to Paige “That poor fellow, still made to walk these grounds in his twilight years”

Paige smirked and whispered her response “My dear Walter, have you learned nothing from me over the years, that manservant is a military man, those stairs may slow him down a little, but I can assure you, he his far from his twilight years”
The butler opened the coach door, Walter stepped out first followed by Paige. The butler assessed the pair, Paige in particular, when he spoke there was measured judgemental tone in his tone. “Can I help you?”
Walter spoke first, Paige’s focus had drifted to the nearby rose bush, she noticed that some of them had been pruned recently, but slightly more erratically than the others, 
“Yes, erm, hello, we’re Prof Paige, I mean this is…” Walter nervously fumbled his words.
“Oh for goodness sake” Paige interrupted, “I’m Professor Paige Turner and this Walter Bishop, we’re expected”

The butler raised an eyebrow as he regarded Paige. 
Prof Paige was 36 years old, she had red hair that, when it wasn’t tied back, would sit on her shoulders. She was lightly freckled checks which made look a few years younger, but it was her attire that gave people, especially the butler, pause, Prof Paige never wore dresses, she would always wear a man’s tailored suit and flat shoes. She despised dresses, she found them cumbersome and difficult to run in if she had to catch a criminal on foot.

“You’re Prof Turner?” the butler replied suspiciously. Prof Paige was still looking at the rosebush when she replied, she spotted a strand of grey hair on one of the thorns. “Correct. That posture of yours — back straight, shoulders relaxed — not a trace of the stiffness of civilian servants. Yes… you’ve drilled before. Fought before.”. No wonder the late Lord Murphy employed you, and made you head of the house staff. He must’ve held you in high regard, but can’t go wrong with a military man.”.

“I’ll never quite get used to how fast she thinks.” Walter thought to himself

The butler was taken aback for a split second before regaining his composure “Please excuse me Miss - I mean Professor Paige, my name is Alfred Greene and as you quite rightly pointed out, I am the head of staff here at Murphy Manor. Inspector Jeffries did indeed inform me that you would be arriving. May offer you some refreshments before…”

Paige interrupted, “Perhaps later, walk with us Alfred, I’d like to see layout of the grounds and speak to the rest of the staff”
Alfred nodded slightly and began to walk with Paige and Walter, Alfred said “Begging your pardon professor, but I found Lord Murphy in study. The police have searched the property, taken fingerprints and concluded that the mur–the incident occurred in the study.”

Paige, now looking at the soil beneath the hedges as they walked, there were some footprints, one set was deeper than the others, replied “A man of your age with no need for spectacles should be more observant.”
Alfred stopped “Begging your pardon again professor but…”
Paige interrupted again “I assume that you have taken more than one life in military career, yes?”
Alfred nodded
“And young Mr Kent was part of your staff, correct?”
Alfred replied, “Yes that’s correct”
Paige’s attention was now drawn to a young woman tending a nearby flowerbed, the very same woman who fled Scotland Yard earlier, Paige noticed that her hand was trembling slightly.
“Professor?!” Walter insisted, he would often have to remind Paige that she was in the middle of conversations with people.
“Hm? Oh, yes, you, Albert. Then tell me this, does someone of Mr Kent’s physique need a rope to kill a man? Would you?”
Mulling over this new train of thought, Alfred replied “It’s Alfred Professor, and no, I don’t suppose Mr Kent would need a rope or any weapon. It did seem strange, given the circumstances”
Paige leant into Alfred a little, “You mean given the late Lord’s promiscuous nature with the younger attractive members of staff” 
Alfred’s silence was enough for Paige. 

Paige continued, “According to the police, there were no real signs of a struggle. They believe Mr Kent’s statement, that he ‘snuck’ into the study and killed Lord Murphy while he slept in his chair. Correct?”
“Yes professor” Alfred replied.
“And where is the study located?” Paige asked
“There, on the left, ground floor” Alfred said pointing to a corner room with a glass balcony door.
“The glass isn’t broken” Walter said, making Paige smirk with pride”
“No” Alfred replied, “The police said the Mr Kent picked the lock, snuck into the study and waited for him to return”
“Was the balcony door locked when you found him?” Paige asked
Alfred nodded
Paige mulled over his response
The three of them continued their tour around the grounds, Alfred showed them the staff quarters that were situated a few yards away from the main house, all the while, the young woman who was tending to the flowers pretending not to watch them.

“Are your quarters here as well Alex?” Paige asked
Alfred replied “No Professor, within my first year of employment Lord Murphy had quarters built for me specifically, it’s situated to the left of the main house”
“Near the study?” Paige asked
“That is correct, professor” Alfred replied
“Where are Mr Kent’s quarters?” Paige enquired
“You passed it on the way here Professor, the small building along the driveway” Alfred said.

“The staff quarters lay a minute’s walk from the manor; Alfred’s private rooms, by contrast, were tucked neatly beside the study.” Paige made a mental note

“That’s quite a distance” Paige thought out loud “Tell me Anton” Paige continued “Did you see or hear anything on the night of the incident? Specifically coming from the grounds”

Alfred replied, “No, not from the grounds. The rest of the Murphy family had all retired for the night at around 10p.m.” he paused, then sighed and relaxed slightly “And then I escorted Lord Murphy to the servants quarters”
With a fraction more warmth than Walter was used to hearing from her, Paige said “Oh no need to prolong the facade, as I said before, Lord Murphy was fond of young ladies, pick up any gossip paper and you’ll find more than a few snippets about his long list of affairs. Now, did you wait for him, after his late night visit?”
Alfred shook his head, “No, as…usual I left him at the door, returned to the manor, locked up and retired for the night. I read for about an hour before going to sleep”
Paige knotted her brow “Interesting”
Paige remained silent for the better part of a minute, her eyes now locked on the young woman tending to the flowers.

“Professor Turner?” Walter said, nudging her shoulder, “What’s interesting?”
Paige blinked back into the conversation.
 “A military man — one trained to wake at the sound of a twig snapping — claims he heard nothing. A six-foot powerhouse supposedly crept across gravel, picked a lock, strangled a man, tidied up, locked the balcony door behind him, and tip-toed back across all this pebbled ground? No, Walter. Had Mr Kent not offered such a ludicrous confession, the police would have arrested you.”
Walter shakes his head “Oh Professor”
Paige’s gaze drifted back to Alfred — not accusing, merely observant. She watched him swallow, noted the faint constriction of his pupils despite the open daylight.
“Unless,” she said mildly, “you slept more deeply than usual that night.”
Alfred’s jaw tightened. He hesitated — just long enough.
“A few drops,” he said at last. “For the pain. Old injuries.”
Paige nodded once.
“Of course.”
Alfred’s jaw tightened. “Professor Turner, I served Lord Murphy for many years. I would never—”
Paige cut in gently. “Yes, yes, you would never harm him. But do humour me. Those footprints by the hedges are fresh — deeper than the others. Someone was out here last night carrying significant weight.”
Alfred stared, caught.
“Now,” Paige said, pointing straight at the young woman pretending not to eavesdrop, “I should like a word with that lady who has been studying us quite intently for the last fifteen minutes.”

## Meeting Claire 

Claire started like a startled rabbit when Paige beckoned her over. Her eyes were red; her fingers shook slightly as she wiped soil on the apron tied around her waist. The faint floral perfume she wore mixed with the smell of damp earth and turned sweetly cloying in the warm morning air.
Walter, overly friendly, gave her a small bow.
 Claire curtsied automatically — the kind of curtsy that said she’d had to do this far too many times in far too few years.
“Miss Summers,” Paige said, tone crisp but not unkind, “your hands are trembling. Either you are cold — which would make you the first person in England to feel a chill on a thirty-degree day.”
Claire swallowed. “I— I didn’t expect guests, Professor.”
“You know me?” Paige tilted her head as she asked
Claire nodded, “I’ve read about all of your cases, professor. Saving the Italian ballerina was…”
”Well if we left it to the police the poor girl would still be looked up beneath Florence’s beautiful Duomo to this day,” Paige interrupted. 
“How did you…” Claire was about to ask.
“Solve the riddles?” Paige interrupted “Simply a mix of astrology and some knowledge of Van Buren's paintings” Paige said proudly.
Alfred stepped in, trying to soften the moment.
 “Claire, they’re simply here to understand what happened the night Lord Murphy died.”
Claire’s lips tightened. “Right. Of course.”
Paige adjusted her cuffs. Paige stepped forward and placed her hand on Claire’s shoulder, the act of compassion caught Walter by surprise.

“Miss Summers, let us not waste one another’s time. Your lover sits in a jail cell awaiting a fate that he does not deserve, but will certainly suffer if you don’t tell precisely what happened that night. And before you ask ‘How could you possibly know that we were lovers?’ we saw you leaving Scotland Yard earlier and you were...”
Paige trailed off, she could feel someone watching her, she turned to look at the house and caught a glimpse of someone, she didn’t see their face, but the height and build suggested it was a man. They fled from the window after being caught by Paige.
“You were at Scotland Yard?” Alfred asked, “I thought that you were at the post office collecting your missing seed shipment?”
“I was…I mean, I did” Claire said  gesturing to the opened box on the grass behind her, she continued “But, Oh Alfred, I had to see him”
Alfred shook his head solemnly, “It’s best that you cooperate with the professor” he said, with a hint of fatherly fondness.
Paige picked up the small cardboard seed box, it had a number of small packets inside, each had pictures of flowers that neither Paige nor Walter had ever seen.

Perhaps to induce some self-soothing, Claire said, “I get them delivered every few months, depending on the season. He likes… I mean Lord Murphy liked to have flowers that no one else in the country had. As you can see, these seeds come from all over the world, Peru, India, and various parts of Africa.”
“You’re an expert botanist?” Walter asked, hoping his tone would ease Claire a little.
“Hardly” Claire replied with a hint of a smile, she was slightly more relaxed now, “I have six more months before I become fully certified, then I can finally leave this place and head to America…but I..don’t know, I mean with all that’s happened”
Claire’s composure cracked.
“You don’t understand,” she whispered. “Julian was just…protecting me, but he’s kind.”
Paige softened, only slightly.
 “Oh, I know. But I would like you to recount the events of that night, Julian’s life depends on it.”
Claire looked between Paige and Walter, unsure whether she was being reassured or threatened.
Paige continued:
 “What happened that night Miss Summers?”
Claire froze.

“Lord Murphy, he…came to my room” she replied, meekly.
Alfred clenched his jaw, a response that was not lost on Paige
“Was this the first time?” Paige asked
Claire fixed her gaze on the floor and shook her head, then glanced at Alfred. “No, he…he came here often. And we…”
Paige raised her hand, “No need for graphic details Miss Summers, I suspect that any affection on his part was not reciprocated on yours? The way that you’re staring at the floor suggests that any feelings were not mutual”
Claire’s eyes began to fill with tears, she clenched her fists and fought them back. “No, he said that he would fully fund my studies and travels. I just had to…” Claire couldn’t bring herself to finish that sentence, “Please don’t think less of me Professor, I don’t come from a wealthy family”.
“The professor is only interested in the facts Claire, please go on” Walter said reassuringly.
Claire continued, “On that night, I had finished supper at around 8pm. And remained in my room all night. Then he… Lord Murphy came to my room, I could hear Alfred with him.”
As Claire looked at Alfred, a look of shame flashed across his eyes. 
“Lord Murphy…” Claire continued

Paige sensing that once again that they were being watched, raised her hand “Come, let us continue this conversation in your room Miss Summers, it’s dreadfully hot today isn’t it?”
Claire blinked her tears away and nodded, “Of course, it’s this way”.
The four of them made their way to the servants quarters. They entered the small building into the main hall, Claire’s room was the first one on the right. They entered the room, it seemed much more opulent than what a servants quarters would normally look like.
Paige took a moment, she could smell the faintest trace of cinnamon in the air.
There was a freshly made double bed with expensive luxury sheets, shelves filled with books about botany, ranging from daily care, toxic flowers and how to handle them, and new discoveries from all over the world. 
Against the opposite wall was a chest of drawers and next to it stood a white wardrobe, hanging on the outside was a white nightgown. Paige observed tiny flecks of yellow around the collar, as she drew closer to it she realised she had found the source of the cinnamon scent.
A small writing desk and chair faced the window, which overlooked a small flowerbed below the wind, the Manor and the entire garden could be seen from it. On the desk was a writing set, Paige could see that the paper and pens were of a high quality, far too expensive for a botanist to afford. Every item on the desk was placed neatly with precision. Paige commented “You’re very organised Miss Summers, my students would do well to follow your example.”

Claire blushed a little, “Thank you professor”
“Oh dear” Paige said looking at the floor near the desk, there lay a small shard of broken glass, Paige carefully picked it up and examined it carefully, “You must’ve knocked over a vase. Frustrating isn’t it? Trying to clean up broken glass, you always miss at least one piece.”
Paige noticed Claire’s eyes widened, “Yes, I’m very clumsy, I knocked over a vase. I must’ve missed that piece”

Paige regarded Claire for a moment “Indeed. Please continue. Lord Murphy came to your room”
Claire recomposed herself she said, voice thin, “Lord Murphy came to my quarters. He— he said he would help fund my research trip if I… if I was agreeable. So we…”
Paige’s disgust was mild but visible.

“Then,” Claire hesitated, “Then he left and went back to the manor”
“What time did he leave?” Paige asked.
“Around 11p.m I think” Claire stuttered
“And after Lord Murphy left, then what happened?” Paige asked
“I–I…I went straight to bed” Claire said
“I see, and did you hear anything?” Paige pressed.
Claire looked visibly uncomfortable. “Um, no, no I slept all night.” she replied

“Nothing? Are you sure?” Paige pressed again.
“I said I didn’t hear anything” Claire snapped.
A slightly stunned fell over the group.
“I’m sorry professor, I’m just very upset about Julian” Claire said.
“Of course, speaking of Julian, he’s quite the impressive figure isn’t he? I would wager that he could easily end the life of any man who got on his wrong side” Paige said.
Claire’s eyes flared “He’s not like that he wouldn’t…”
“Kill anyone? Paige interrupted, “No Miss Summers, I don’t believe that he would deliberately cause harm to another person either. And yet he sits in a jail cell awaiting his fate.”

Claire remained silent, not making eye contact.
Paige continued “So you slept, without hearing a sound all night. And when you awoke?”
Claire kept glancing toward the manor windows — as if someone might be watching.
Claire straightened, “When I woke up, I bathed and dressed ready for work. I made my way to the manor. Mrs Clarke was outside talking to the police”
“Mrs Clarke?” Paige asked
“Mrs Emma Clarke, she’s the cook” Alfred
“She told you what happened to Lord Murphy?” Paige asked.

Claire nodded.

“What did you say?” Paige asked
“Sorry?” Paige answered confused
“When Mrs Clarke told you about the murder of your employer and the hands of your lover. What did you say?” Paige pressed.
Claire froze and eventually stuttered “I don’t– I mean, I was in shock. I said that I couldn’t believe it. I couldn’t believe that Julian would do such a thing.”
“Thank you Miss Summers, you have been very helpful” Paige said nodding slightly. “I’m going to meet the Murphy family. I trust that I can call on you again, should I need your assistance?” Paige continued.
“Of course professor” Claire replied

They all turned to leave the room, Paige stopped near the wardrobe and gesture to the white nightgown, “I didn’t notice any cinnamon trees in your exotic garden”
“Cinnamon?” Claire said nervously, “I–I had some of Mrs Clarke’s apple pie, she likes to put extra cinnamon on it, I must’ve got some on my gown” 
Paige noticed that Alred furrowed his eyebrows
“Oh? When was that?” Paige asked.
“Mrs Clarke made it on the night of the incident” Alfred replied
Paige looked perplexed “The night before last? Forgive me, I’m not an expert, but cinnamon is brown not yellow, correct?”

Claire looked nervously at the gown and began to twist her apron in her hands “It must’ve faded over time, I’ll get it washed”, she continued.
“Of course, thank you again Miss Summers” Paige said smiling.

As they left, Claire went back to her work. Paige instructed Alfred to prepare the family for her arrival. As he left she spoke to Walter, barely containing her excitement.
“Oh Walter, what a wonderful brain teaser you’ve brought me!” Paige said with childlike glee.
“I hardly think that a murder is something to get excited about, professor, but you’re very…unconventional” Walter replied.
Paige scoffed “You mean dull!”
Walter smiled, “You’re onto something I take it?” 
Paige was looking down the driveway towards Julian’s quarters, then she started to jump up and down on the pebbles beneath her feet, then she took huge strides back and forth, stomping onto the pebbles.

Walter looked confused “Professor, PROFESSOR, what on earth are you doing?” Walter had to raise his voice.
Paige spun on her heels, nearly falling “Earth! Precisely Walter, earth, soil!”
Walter looked more confused than ever.

Paige threw her arms in the air in frustration “Do you see any soil beneath us right now?” Paige said.
Walter was about to reply, but knew better try to get a word in when Paige had that look in her eye.
Paige continued “No! No soil. There’s only pebbles from outside the servants quarters all the way to the main gate, past Julian’s quarters! Therefore…” Paige looked expectantly at Walter.
“Well therefore…” Walter tried to answer.

“Therefore…” Paige interrupted “how can a man as big as Julian walk past the servants quarters, Claire’s specifically, without making a sound on these pebbles, loud enough that she wouldn’t have heard anything? Physics Walter, physics!”
“Forgive me professor, but a good solicitor would cry conjecture” Walter replied.
Paige paused, “Granted, but only in isolation. That piece of glass for example, that came from no vase, I know a Baccarat when I see one.”
Walter knotted his brow “It could just mean that she or Lord Murphy broke a glass”
Paige retorted immediately “Then why lie? She already told us what Lord Murphy was up to in his late night visits. She felt so much shame that she actually asked me not to think less of her, remember? So again, why lie about the glass?”
Walter nodded “I see your point”

Paige continued “There were a few things that were more than strange, she seemed more than certain that Julian wouldn’t hurt anyone. There was the nonsense about the cinnamon”
Walter looked perplexed “She admitted that she needed to wash the gown, she was clearly upset so she must have forgotten”
“HA!” Paige yelled “Forgotten? You saw how neat that room was, everything in its place. No Walter, she didn’t forget, she just didn’t notice.”
Walter sighed, “Very well Professor, we should go” he paused and noticed that Paige had trained her gaze towards the manor “Professor” he asked.
“She’s afraid of that family Walter, deathly afraid. When have you ever known someone to have perfect memory recall of a conversation that they had over 24 hours ago, whilst suffering from shock?” Paige whispered.
“I have to say, never” Walter joined Paige in her gaze.
“Never indeed, something foul happened in her room Walter. I think we can safely say that Julian is an innocent man. In the meantime, into the vipers pit we go”
With that, they headed towards the house.
As they approached they could see that the Murphys were watching them through the window
